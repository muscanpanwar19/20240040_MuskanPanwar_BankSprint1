1. SCOPE & EXCLUSION OF REDUNDANT UNIT TEST CONDITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In accordance with the IT7742 Assessment 3 Marking Rubric, these Behavior-Driven
Development (BDD) scenarios focus exclusively on the new Sprint 3 business logic:
  - Intra-account money transfers between a single customer's owned accounts
    (Everyday, Investment, and Omni).
  - Account-specific failure states (Everyday zero-overdraft rejection,
    Investment insufficient balance, and Omni overdraft limit breaches).
  - Bank Staff Benefit enforcement (50% penalty fee discount: $5.00 vs $10.00).
  - Transfer security and input integrity (no cross-customer transfers, no
    identical source/destination transfers, positive transfer amounts only).

Redundant unit test conditions already verified in Sprint 1 and Sprint 2
(such as standalone Deposit, standalone Withdraw, standalone Interest
calculation, and Customer CRUD operations) are intentionally excluded.
